WIBEON AI - Account Deletion Request

Last updated: September 3, 2026

WIBEON AI respects your privacy and provides users with a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your WIBEON AI account:
  1. Send an account deletion request using the contact method provided on the official WIBEON AI website or in the application.
  2. Include the email address or username associated with your WIBEON AI account.
  3. We may request information necessary to verify that the request belongs to the account owner.

What Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is approved and processed, we intend to delete or anonymize personal information associated with the account, including account and profile information, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

User-generated content and communication data associated with the account may also be deleted or anonymized where applicable and technically possible.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period when required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary and will be deleted or anonymized when the applicable retention requirement ends.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after the request is verified. The exact processing time may depend on the nature of the request and applicable legal requirements.

Important Information

Account deletion may be permanent and may result in loss of access to your account, messages, profile information, and other account-related content.

Contact

For account deletion requests or questions about your personal data, please contact the WIBEON AI support team through the official WIBEON AI website or the contact information provided with the application.
