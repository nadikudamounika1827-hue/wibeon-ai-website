WIBEON AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

WIBEON AI ("WIBEON AI", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the WIBEON AI mobile application and related services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, WIBEON AI may collect:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, username, and profile information.
  • Information you provide in your profile or while communicating with other users.
  • Messages and other content you choose to send through the application.
  • Language preferences and translation-related information required to provide translation features.
  • Information related to meetings, events, or communication features that you choose to create or participate in.
  • Photos, camera images, microphone or other device information when you voluntarily use features that require these permissions.
  • Technical information such as device type, operating system, application version, and diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide messaging and communication features.
  • Provide language detection and translation services.
  • Provide meetings, events, voice, and related communication features.
  • Improve application performance, reliability, and security.
  • Detect and help prevent spam, scams, abuse, malicious links, and other harmful activity.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements.

3. Translation and AI Features

WIBEON AI provides AI-assisted communication and translation features. Content submitted to features that require AI processing may be processed to provide the requested functionality. We aim to process information only as necessary to provide and improve the relevant service.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or technology providers when necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or provide WIBEON AI features, or when required by law.

5. Messages and User Content

Messages and content you submit are used to provide communication and related application features. You should avoid sending highly sensitive personal information through the service unless necessary.

6. Camera, Microphone and Photos

Certain features may request access to your camera, microphone, or photos. These permissions are used only when you choose to use features that require them. You can manage permissions through your device settings.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in the application, you may be able to update your profile information, manage device permissions, and request deletion of your account or personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

WIBEON AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us.

11. Third-Party Services

WIBEON AI may use third-party services to provide hosting, authentication, analytics, AI processing, security, communications, or other application functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or WIBEON AI, please contact the WIBEON AI support team through the contact information provided with the application or its official website.
